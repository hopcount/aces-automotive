About Aces Automotive

Your local car mechanic in Dandenong

Your search for a local car mechanic in Dandenong ends at Aces Automotive. We're car mechanic experts offering a range of services to cater to all your car repair and maintenance needs — from brushing the brake plate to oiling the engine, changing wheels and aligning them to perfection, repairing an accidental car, or offering preventive service for major issues.

The mechanics on our team are honed to perfection through extensive exposure to a wide range of client and car-repair projects. We provide the best possible service to enhance the value of your vehicle and add years to its life. Besides making a car service plan to suit your individual budget, we also extend a guarantee on all our services.

We are a team of fully qualified and experienced car mechanics, well-equipped to perform a range of car repair jobs — servicing, mechanical repairs, logbook servicing, fleet management, and other preventive and corrective repair services, all at the most affordable price.
