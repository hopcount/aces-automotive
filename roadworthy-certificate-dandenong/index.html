Roadworthy Certificate Dandenong

We inspect your car to make sure it's safe to drive on the road, checking its roadworthiness before issuing a roadworthy certificate. We're qualified to offer Roadworthy Dandenong only after assuring the vehicle is 100% safe.

At Aces Automotive we're a team of expert roadworthy testers, putting our knowledge and skills to work assessing your vehicle against Dandenong's roadworthiness requirements.

A roadworthy certificate is required when selling a car, buying a used car, or re-registering a vehicle — and it's a good measure of a car's overall safety.

What's included in a roadworthy certificate?

  • Full inspection of tyres, lights, brakes, suspension and structure
  • Engine assessment (horn, seatbelts, door latches, windscreen etc.)
  • Certificate issued by a licensed, VACC-accredited tester
  • Pre-inspection check available before your official test

Why come to us for your roadworthy certificate?

We're licensed to inspect and issue roadworthy certificates for cars of all makes and models, with extensive experience across roadworthy inspections. Whether you're buying or selling, we're a trusted provider — our capability, experience and evaluation skills are unquestionable.
